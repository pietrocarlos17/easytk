Bra tee with removable pads and Climacool technology — sold as a 2-piece set with matching leggings.

Bold style meets practical performance with the adidas Originals Sport Superstar 2-Piece Set. Designed for those who thrive on versatility, this kit blurs the boundaries between gym and street with a cropped bra tee and form-fitting leggings that move with you.

The statement-making bra tee features piping details and stitch-applied 3-Stripes, bringing iconic adidas flair. A crew neckline and heat-transfer Trefoil branding add a modern touch, while removable pads allow you to adjust coverage to suit your needs. Adiflex material delivers support for a powerful feel.

Climacool technology wicks and disperses sweat across both pieces, keeping you cool, dry, and distraction-free through every rep. The matching leggings complete the look with the same piping details and a sleek silhouette built for performance.

Whether you're powering through training or stepping out into everyday life, this 2-piece set is your go-to for confidence and ease. Make your movement count with adidas.

Washing Instructions

Extra Care Information

  • Use mild detergent only
  • Wash and iron inside out
