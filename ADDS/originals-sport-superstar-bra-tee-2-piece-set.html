Cropped tee and sports bra set with Climacool technology for cool, dry performance.

The adidas Originals Sport Superstar Bra Tee 2-Piece Set pairs a cropped graphic tee with a supportive medium-support sports bra — designed for those who move between the gym, the streets, and everywhere in between.

The relaxed cropped tee features a bold Trefoil print and a boxy silhouette, adding a casual edge to any activewear look. Lightweight fabric keeps you comfortable through every move.

The matching sports bra delivers medium support with Climacool technology, wicking sweat and dispersing heat so you stay cool and dry. Removable pads and a fully bonded cradle construction give you the freedom to customise your coverage.

Signature 3-Stripes and heat-transfer Trefoil branding tie the two pieces together for a cohesive, sport-inspired look that works as hard as you do.
